Hi! I am a PhD student in [Computer Science](https://cs.illinois.edu) at [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu) since 2022 fall. I am co-advised by [Prof. Reyhaneh Jabbarvand](https://reyhaneh.cs.illinois.edu) and [Prof. Darko Marinov](https://mir.cs.illinois.edu/marinov/). 

My research interests:
- AI coding agents, agentic AI systems, LLM evaluation and code reasoning, LLM fine-tuning and post-training.
- AI and software engineering, synthetic data generation, software testing (flaky test detection & repair, mutation testing), and genetic algorithms.

My work has been published at multiple top SE venues (FSE, ICSE, ISSTA, TSE, etc.).
